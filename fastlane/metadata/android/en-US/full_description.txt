Galiciana de Peto is an independent app for researching digitized publications from Galiciana, the Digital Library of Galicia.

Search the newspaper archive, read original pages with OCR highlights, and organize findings with bookmarks, folders and reference notes. Export and restore your research data as a ZIP backup.

Document searches and viewing require an internet connection to Galiciana. Bookmarks and notes are stored on the device. The rights of each document are listed in its source record.
